    Cat Adoption Policy

    By adopting this cat, the adopter agrees to provide proper food, fresh water, safe shelter, veterinary care, and a loving, permanent home. The adopter accepts full responsibility for the cat's health, safety, and well-being and agrees not to neglect, abuse, abandon, sell, or otherwise transfer ownership of the cat without permission. This adoption is intended to be a lifelong commitment. If at any time the adopter is no longer able or willing to care for the cat, the cat must be returned to the original owner/rescue organization and may not be rehomed, surrendered, sold, or given to another person. Acceptance of the cat constitutes agreement to all terms of this policy.

    A Fur Parent Reminder

    1. Provide fresh food and clean water every day.
    2. Keep your cat safe, healthy, and up to date with veterinary care.
    3. Give your cat love, attention, and daily interaction.
    4. Never abandon, neglect, or mistreat your cat.
    5. If you can no longer care for your cat, please return the cat to us rather than rehoming or surrendering it elsewhere. 🐾
